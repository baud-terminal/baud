# ORB trail -- research notes (2026-09-26)

Scratch research; nothing in baud touched. Sources and hashes: `pins.txt`.
Harness: `harness/main.swift` + `harness/orb.metal` (reads baud's working-tree
shaders.metal read-only for glyph_vertex, EffectU, fx_* and the fire).
Outputs: `run-fast.out`, `run-safe.out`, `png/*.png`.

## Found in the tree while researching

An uncommitted refactor was in progress: `baud-render/src/effect.rs`, which
contains `Effect { Fire }`, `Ignition`, `Vfx`, `Particle`, and an `EffectU`
made of a 24-float prefix (reach at [23]) plus `k[8]`. `Kind::Band` is 4 and
`Kind::Particle` is 5. The orb is designed to plug into that, and it needs
three small extensions, listed below.

## Design in one paragraph

A glowing ball rides kitty's quad as a fifth chased point, the **lead**, on
TAU_FAST (0.1 s). It is retargeted from wherever it is, so it is continuous
under any retarget. Its wake is a spine-bright ribbon inside kitty's quad,
falling off with distance from the ball. A Plummer halo surrounds it. It lands
into the cursor: it shrinks and fades over 0.12 s, a ring expands from 2.2 R to
1.5 cells over 0.2 s, and five motes orbit it in a tilted ring and collapse
with it.

The shader evaluates only functions of the pixel. Every function of time is
evaluated once in Rust and carried in `k[8]`: lead.xy, R_now, glow, ring.xy,
ring_r and ring_a. There is no noise, no hash, no log and no trig in the
shader.

## Measured

- **GPU against the CPU reference.** The scenes are across, diagonal,
  retarget and bar cursor. Each ran at 9 ages on dark, light and glass
  grounds, 108 frames in all.
  - Worst channel difference: 1 step.
  - Pixels one step off: 10 with fast math, 13 with safe/precise.
  - Pixels storing colour past their alpha: 0.
  - `MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1` reported nothing.
- **Reach of 2.0 cells.** Just inside the cut, the field is at most 0.184/255.
  Just past it, the uncut field is at most 0.165/255. Both maxima occur in the
  retarget scene.
- **Retarget continuity.** Over ±1e-4 s, the lead moves 2.43 px and the
  corners move 1.88 px. This is continuous in position; the high speed comes
  from the start of kitty's exponential. The glow goes 0.9685 -> 0.9690.
- **Landing and end.** It lands at 0.100-0.141 s and ends at 0.300-0.341 s,
  which is 36-41 frames at 120 Hz.
- **Cost over the same bands** (113,526 px, a diagonal at 0.05 s):
  - Per draw: orb 4.4 us against fire 12.4 us in one session, and 21.0
    against 59.1 in another. The GPU clock state differed between sessions.
  - The ratio is 2.8x in both.
- **Flashes.** A flash is WCAG's pair of opposing changes of at least 0.1
  relative luminance, counted per pixel at 240 Hz.
  - With the ring starting at R, a pixel beside the hole flashed 3 times in
    one ignition: orb on, shrink, ring.
  - With the ring starting at 2.2 R, at most 2 flashes.
  - Pixels that flash at all over a whole ignition: about 7.8k device px,
    about 1.9k CSS px², which is 8.5-9.6% of WCAG's 25%-of-10° area
    (21,824 CSS px²).
