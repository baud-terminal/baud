# What a key typed with Shift sends, the same from either keyboard: the
# Wayland driver's, on the pinned US keymap, and the macOS app's, on the
# US layout. Each row is read by both drivers' tests.
#
#   chord  key  typed | mode | sent
#
# chord: shift, or ctrl+shift. key: the key's unshifted character on the
# US layout, typed: what it types with the chord's Shift, both as a
# character or the word "space". mode: legacy (power-on), kitty=N (the
# flags CSI > N u pushes) or mok=N (modifyOtherKeys N, CSI > 4 ; N m).
# sent: the bytes, \e for ESC and \xNN for any byte.
shift a A | legacy | A
shift a A | kitty=1 | A
shift a A | kitty=8 | \e[97;2u
shift a A | kitty=11 | \e[97;2u
shift a A | kitty=12 | \e[97:65;2u
shift a A | kitty=24 | \e[97;2;65u
shift a A | mok=1 | A
shift a A | mok=2 | \e[27;2;65~
shift 1 ! | legacy | !
shift 1 ! | kitty=1 | !
shift 1 ! | kitty=8 | \e[49;2u
shift 1 ! | kitty=12 | \e[49:33;2u
shift 1 ! | kitty=24 | \e[49;2;33u
shift 1 ! | mok=2 | \e[27;2;33~
shift space space | legacy | \x20
shift space space | kitty=8 | \e[32;2u
ctrl+shift a A | legacy | \x01
ctrl+shift a A | kitty=8 | \e[97;6u
ctrl+shift a A | kitty=12 | \e[97:65;6u
ctrl+shift a A | mok=2 | \e[27;6;65~
ctrl+shift 2 @ | legacy | \x00
